🎬 Movies

Ad-supported streamers and public-domain libraries that rival paid services.

Tubi

Ad-supported

Best for: Hollywood catalog depth, cult classics, anime, foreign films.

Premium move: Make a free account so your watchlist and resume-watching sync — Tubi then behaves like Netflix, just with short ad breaks.

tubitv.com

Pluto TV

Live + on-demand

Best for: Linear "channel surfing" — 250+ themed channels (action, horror, classics).

Premium move: Pin your 6 favorite channels and treat the guide as your cable replacement; jump to on-demand only when nothing's on.

pluto.tv

Plex Free Movies

Library + AVOD

Best for: A single library that mixes free streaming with your own media.

Premium move: Use Plex's universal Watchlist + Discover to track what's on every service at once, then play the free ones in-app.

plex.tv/watch-free

Kanopy

Library card

Best for: Criterion-tier indie, arthouse, and award-winning international cinema.

Premium move: Log in with your public library or university card — you get a monthly credit pool, no ads, and 4K on supported titles.

kanopy.com

Internet Archive Feature Films

Public domain

Best for: Classic Hollywood, noir, silent films, restored prints.

Premium move: Download originals in MP4 for offline viewing — fully legal, no DRM, perfect for travel.

archive.org/details/feature_films

📺 TV Shows

Catch-up portals from major networks plus ad-supported on-demand giants.

The Roku Channel

Free, web + app

Best for: Network sitcoms, reality TV, and exclusive Roku Originals.

Premium move: Use the "Live TV" guide for free news/sports channels; add favorites to mimic a DVR shelf.

therokuchannel.roku.com

Freevee (Amazon)

Amazon-free tier

Best for: Bingeable network drama, full procedural runs (Bosch spin-offs etc.).

Premium move: Sign in with any Amazon account so progress syncs across web, Fire TV, and phones like Prime.

amazon.com/adlp/freevee

PBS

Public broadcaster

Best for: Masterpiece dramas, Frontline, NOVA, kids' programming.

Premium move: Some episodes go member-only — pair with a small local PBS membership for unlimited library access.

pbs.org

CW (free with ads)

Network site

Best for: Current-season episodes of CW dramas the day after they air.

Premium move: No login needed — bookmark just your shows' pages so it acts like a personal episode tracker.

cwtv.com

Crackle

Ad-supported

Best for: Older Sony shows, action originals, and complete series in one place.

Premium move: Sort by "Just Added" weekly; queue full series so the autoplay carries you binge-style.

crackle.com

🎵 Music

From free Spotify tiers to artist-owned platforms and curated radio.

Spotify Free

Web + app

Best for: Discovery — algorithmic playlists, Daily Mixes, Discover Weekly.

Premium move: On desktop you can pick any track on-demand for free — pin Spotify as a web app and use it like the paid tier.

spotify.com

YouTube Music

Ad-supported

Best for: Rare live sets, remixes, and the world's deepest catalog of uploads.

Premium move: Build your own "Liked" library; use the YouTube Music web app fullscreen for a clean now-playing experience.

music.youtube.com

Bandcamp

Artist-direct

Best for: Indie, experimental, and supporting artists with full-stream previews.

Premium move: Follow artists you love — every release surfaces in your feed; "name your price" often = free downloads.

bandcamp.com

SoundCloud

Free tier

Best for: New artists, DJ mixes, remix culture, podcasts-as-music.

Premium move: Follow 20–30 tastemakers; the "Stream" feed becomes a real-time underground radio.

soundcloud.com

Radio Garden

Worldwide radio

Best for: Live radio from any city on Earth — spin the globe, drop in.

Premium move: Save 5 cities in different time zones — instant mood/lighting changes through the day.

radio.garden

🎙️ Podcasts

Open ecosystems — no platform lock-in, every show, every device.

Spotify Podcasts

Web + app

Best for: Mainstream exclusives (Rogan, Smartless, NYT shows) in one app.

Premium move: Set playback to 1.2× and use "Your Episodes" as a unified queue across devices.

open.spotify.com

Pocket Casts (Web)

Free web app

Best for: Power-user features: silence trimming, volume boost, custom playlists.

Premium move: Build a "Up Next" filter for unplayed episodes <30 min — perfect commute queue auto-builder.

play.pocketcasts.com

Apple Podcasts (Web)

Web player

Best for: Largest open catalog + clean discovery charts.

Premium move: Use Apple as your discovery layer, then copy the RSS feed into your real player to keep history portable.

podcasts.apple.com

Overcast (Web Sync)

Free, ad-supported

Best for: Smart Speed + Voice Boost — chops dead air, audibly faster shows.

Premium move: Star episodes worth re-listening; your starred list is a personal "greatest hits" library.

overcast.fm

NPR / BBC Sounds

Public radio

Best for: Journalism, deep-reporting, and ad-light long-form audio.

Premium move: Subscribe to one daily (Up First) + one weekly deep-dive (This American Life / In Our Time) — a balanced news diet.

npr.org · bbc.co.uk/sounds

📚 Books

Library cards turn into the most under-rated subscription you'll ever own.

Libby (OverDrive)

Library card

Best for: Brand-new bestsellers and audiobooks via your local library.

Premium move: Link 2–3 library cards (home + a partner city) — wait times effectively disappear.

libbyapp.com

Project Gutenberg

Public domain

Best for: 70,000+ classics, EPUB/Kindle-ready, no account ever.

Premium move: Build a "to-read classics" folder on your e-reader; sync once and never need wifi again.

gutenberg.org

Standard Ebooks

Curated PD

Best for: Beautifully typeset, proofread versions of public-domain books.

Premium move: Use this instead of Gutenberg for any classic — gets you a Penguin Classics-grade reading experience for free.

standardebooks.org

Open Library (Archive.org)

1-hour loans

Best for: Out-of-print, academic, and hard-to-find titles.

Premium move: Use the "Read Aloud" feature to convert any borrowed book into a free audiobook in-browser.

openlibrary.org

LibriVox

Audiobooks

Best for: Free audiobooks of public-domain literature, read by volunteers.

Premium move: Filter by 4+ star readers; download chapters as MP3 so it works in any podcast app.

librivox.org

Keep forever

Best for: AAA and indie titles given away every Thursday — yours permanently.

Premium move: Set a Thursday calendar reminder; in a year you'll have a $1,000+ library at $0.

store.epicgames.com/free-games

Steam — Free to Play

F2P + demos

Best for: Multiplayer giants (Dota, CS2, Warframe) + the "Next Fest" demo events.

Premium move: Wishlist demos during Next Fest — Steam emails you when full games launch on sale.

store.steampowered.com

itch.io

Indie

Best for: Wildly original indie games, jam entries, and free experimental projects.

Premium move: Browse the "Free" tag sorted by Top — a guided tour of the best zero-cost indies on the web.

itch.io

GOG — Free Games

DRM-free

Best for: Classic PC games that "just work" — fully DRM-free downloads.

Premium move: Grab giveaways during seasonal sales and back up installers offline — they'll run forever.

gog.com/games?price=free

Internet Archive — Console Library

In-browser

Best for: Playing retro classics (DOS, Atari, arcade) right in the browser.

Premium move: Bookmark a personal "arcade" page of 10 favorites — your own free retro console.

archive.org/details/internetarcade

🧠 Learning

Ivy-grade courses, language tools, and skill workshops — at $0.

Khan Academy

K–college

Best for: Math, science, SAT/AP prep, economics — structured from zero to advanced.

Premium move: Pick a "Course Challenge" first to skip what you already know; the system custom-builds your path.

khanacademy.org

MIT OpenCourseWare

University-level

Best for: Full MIT undergrad & grad courses — slides, problem sets, lecture videos.

Premium move: Pick one course per semester, follow the actual syllabus week-by-week — it's a free MIT audit.

ocw.mit.edu

Coursera (Audit Mode)

Audit free

Best for: Stanford, Yale, Google, IBM courses — videos and readings free, certificate optional.

Premium move: On enrollment, click "Audit the course" to skip the paywall and access every lecture.

coursera.org

YouTube EDU (CrashCourse, 3Blue1Brown, Veritasium)

Curated channels

Best for: Visually rich, 10–20 min explainers across every subject.

Premium move: Build a "Learn" playlist; treat it like a daily class — one episode + one page of notes = course-grade outcome.

youtube.com/@crashcourse

Duolingo

Languages

Best for: Daily language practice across 40+ languages with streak gamification.

Premium move: Combine with free Coffee Break Languages podcasts to add real conversational context.

duolingo.com
